# ER Bed Board

A visual, shared ER bed board (`er-board.html`), published as a claude.ai artifact.

- Bed cards you can rename, add, remove and drag into your floor layout ("Move beds").
- Tap a bed to open its chart: name/initials, age, sex, chief complaint, arrival time (time in ER), vitals (BP, HR, RR, Temp, SpO2).
- Diagnostics and therapeutics checklists. Nurses tick items; each tick shows who did it and when.
- Disposition: MGH (with take-home meds checklist), Admit, Transfer, HAMA.
- Patient leaving: Discharged / Admitted / Transferred / HAMA / Absconded buttons at the end of the edit panel. After confirming, the chart is saved to the Discharge list and the bed is emptied. Records can be searched, filtered, put back in their bed, or deleted.
- Live sync: everyone with edit access sees changes within seconds.

Patient data lives in the artifact's database, not in this repository.
